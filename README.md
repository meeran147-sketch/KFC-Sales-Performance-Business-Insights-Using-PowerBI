# KFC-Sales-Performance-Business-Insights-Using-PowerBI
## Project objective
The objective of this project is to design an interactive Power BI dashboard that provides a comprehensive analysis of KFC sales performance across products, regions, cities, sales channels, customer demographics, and promotional campaigns. The dashboard aims to help stakeholders monitor key business metrics such as total revenue, total sales, top-performing products, regional performance, and customer spending behavior.By transforming raw sales data into meaningful visual insights, this project enables data-driven decision-making, identifies sales trends and customer preferences, and highlights opportunities to improve marketing strategies, product performance, and overall business growth.

## Questions
- What is the total revenue and total sales generated across all KFC outlets?
- Which product generates the highest sales revenue?
- Which region contributes the most to overall sales?
- How do sales vary across different product categories?
- Which city records the highest sales performance?
- What is the sales distribution across different order channels (Delivery, Dine-in, and Take-away)?
- How do promotional campaigns influence product sales?
- What is the spending distribution between male and female customers?
- Which products contribute the least to total sales and require improvement?
- How do filters such as city, gender, and product category affect overall sales performance?
- Which customer segment contributes the highest revenue?
- What are the top five best-selling products based on total sales?
- Which promotion type generates the highest product demand?
- What are the key performance indicators (KPIs) for monitoring overall business performance?
- What actionable insights can be derived to improve sales, customer engagement, and business growth?

### Process
- Collected KFC sales data from the source.
- Cleaned the dataset by removing duplicates and handling missing values.
- Transformed the data using Power Query for consistency.
- Created relationships between tables in the data model.
- Developed DAX measures for KPIs such as Total Revenue, Total Sales, and Top Product Sales.
- Designed an interactive Power BI dashboard with KPI cards, charts, tables, and slicers.
- Added filters for Category, Gender, and City to enable dynamic analysis.
- Analyzed sales performance by product, region, promotion type, customer gender, and sales channel.
- Validated the dashboard to ensure data accuracy and consistent results.
- Generated business insights to support data-driven decision-making and improve sales performance.

### Project Insights
- Identified the top-performing products that contribute the highest revenue and sales.
- Analyzed regional and city-wise sales performance to identify high- and low-performing markets.
- Evaluated the effectiveness of promotional campaigns on overall sales and customer purchases.
- Compared customer spending patterns based on gender to understand customer behavior.
- Measured the contribution of different sales channels (Dine-in, Delivery, and Takeaway) to total revenue.
- Monitored key business KPIs such as Total Revenue, Total Sales, Total Products, and Total Cities in a single dashboard.
- Used interactive filters to perform detailed analysis by Category, City, and Gender.
- Identified underperforming products and regions to support targeted business improvement strategies.
- Enabled data-driven decision-making through clear, interactive visualizations and real-time business insights.
- Provided actionable recommendations to improve sales performance, optimize promotions, and increase customer engagement.

## Final Conclusion
The KFC Sales Performance Dashboard successfully transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through an interactive and user-friendly Power BI report. By analyzing key metrics such as revenue, product performance, regional sales, customer demographics, sales channels, and promotional campaigns, the dashboard provides a comprehensive view of business performance.The insights help identify top-performing products, high-revenue regions, and customer purchasing trends while highlighting opportunities to improve underperforming areas. With dynamic filters and real-time visualizations, decision-makers can quickly explore data, monitor KPIs, and make informed strategic decisions. Overall, this dashboard demonstrates how Power BI can be used to support data-driven decision-making, optimize business performance, and drive sustainable sales growth.
